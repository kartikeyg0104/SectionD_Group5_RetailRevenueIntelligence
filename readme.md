# Retail Revenue Intelligence

### Unlocking Sales Performance Through Data-Driven Insights

---

##  Project Overview

This project focuses on building an end-to-end **data-driven retail analytics system** to uncover key revenue drivers and support strategic business decisions.

Using a combination of **Python (Jupyter Notebook)** and **Tableau**, raw transactional retail data was transformed into an interactive dashboard solution that enables stakeholders to explore performance across categories, customers, channels, and time.

---

##  Business Problem

Retail businesses often lack a clear understanding of what truly drives revenue across categories, customers, channels, and promotions.

This project answers:

> **Which product categories, locations, and customer segments drive the most revenue and profitability, and how do pricing, discounts, and payment methods influence sales performance over time?**

---

## Business Impact

This analysis enables stakeholders to:

* Optimize **pricing and discount strategies**
* Improve **inventory allocation**
* Identify **high-performing categories and customers**
* Strengthen **customer retention strategies**
* Maintain **balanced omni-channel performance**

---

## Key Insights

* 🥩 **Butchers is the top-performing category** (₹2.08L revenue)
* 🥛 **Milk Products underperform** due to low pricing
* 🎯 **Discounts do NOT increase revenue significantly**
* 📦 **Quantity sold is the strongest revenue driver (r = 0.71)**
* 🌐 **Online & In-Store channels are equally strong**
* 💳 **Payment method has no impact on spending behavior**
